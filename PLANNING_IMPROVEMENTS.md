# Planifier le futur — future improvements

Recorded 17 November 2026 after the Edumapper review. This is a future feature
brief; the comparison feature described here has not been implemented.
Research and reference links: EDUMAPPER_RESEARCH.md.

## First priority: Comparer deux projets

Help a family make a concrete choice using its saved projects and documented
information. Put **Comparer deux projets** beside the saved projects in the
establishment section of Planifier le futur. Let the family choose two projects
from the same education stage, then show a compact side-by-side comparison.

For a collège profile, start with two lycée choices. Keep longer-term formations
available for exploration. Comparing courses at different stages should require
a deliberate choice and clear stage labels, rather than suggesting equivalence.

The first version should show:

| Row | Content |
| --- | --- |
| What you study | Subjects, options, sections or specialities; identify the exact formation rather than only the school. |
| Practical details | Location, public/private status, duration and documented costs. Add commute information only with a reliable basis; location alone must not become an invented journey time. |
| How to enter | Sector assignment, application, tests and published requirements, with source/session. |
| The child's current strengths | Relevant observed subjects and declared interests, described qualitatively. Keep prerequisites separate from strengths and avoid turning grades into an admission percentage. |
| Next step | One useful action per project: an open day, checking an option, reading the procedure or discussing the choice with a teacher. |

Use the existing catalogue and saved-project data first. Every factual detail
should link to its source; absent/unverified information should say **À vérifier**.
Published deadlines and indicative school-year milestones must be distinguishable.
Do not require a larger catalogue, a new account, an external paid service or an
admission-prediction model for this first version.

Keep the existing project/action storage in each app: family/child-scoped in
Claude-FamilyApp, the current child-specific workflow in Claude/site. Account or
child switching must close the previous comparison and never carry its private
notes/choices into another child's view. Reopening planning should preserve saved
projects and their actions. Check the comparison on desktop and mobile.

Acceptance examples:

- A family saves two lycée options and compares their actual sections, location,
  documented entry procedure and next action.
- A missing cost or requirement is shown as À vérifier, with no inferred figure.
- The view connects relevant subject strengths/interests with both projects while
  leaving room for the child's interests and results to change.
- Removing a saved project or changing child/account updates or closes the comparison.

## Next priorities

1. A short interests questionnaire to improve suggestions beyond school grades.
2. Clearer project explanations: what the child would study and the paths onward.
3. A small, useful set of professions linked to interests and study routes.
4. Project-linked open days, official application dates and family actions.
5. Revisit personal post-bac estimates when lycée-level information and a
   defensible data/model basis or supported integration are available. Edumapper's
   own methodology excludes children still at collège from its estimator.

English remains a separate release decision. The current approved work is the
dashboard cleanup, not this future comparison feature.
